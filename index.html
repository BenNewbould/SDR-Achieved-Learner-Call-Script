Past Learner Re-Engagement Call Script

Call flow Objective 1. Introduction 2. Reconnect 3. Progression 4. AI Conversation 5. Other Programmes 6. Information & Callback 7. CRM Notes 8. Referral

Objective of the Call

  • Reconnect with previous Velocity Academy learners.
  • Understand what has happened since they completed their apprenticeship.
  • Identify promotions, increased responsibility or employer moves.
  • Explore further training and progression opportunities.
  • Introduce the AI & Automation apprenticeship where relevant.
  • Send course information and secure a follow-up call.
  • Where training is not relevant, explore potential referrals.
1

Introduction

SDR

Hi [First Name], it’s [SDR Name] calling from Velocity Academy.

You completed your [Apprenticeship Course] apprenticeship with us while you were working at [Employer].

I’m just getting back in touch with some of our previous learners to see how things have gone since completing their apprenticeship.

Have you got a couple of minutes?

2

Reconnect and Understand Their Progression

SDR

How have things been going since you finished your apprenticeship?

Follow-up questions

  • Are you still working at [Employer]?
  • Have you moved into a different role since completing the apprenticeship?
  • Have you taken on any additional responsibilities?
  • Have you had a promotion or moved into a more senior position?
  • Are you still working within the same area as your apprenticeship?
  • Have you moved to another organisation?
  • Has the apprenticeship helped you progress in the way you expected?

The aim here is to have a genuine conversation rather than immediately moving into another course.

3

Introduce Further Progression

SDR

It sounds like quite a lot has changed since you completed the programme.

Have you thought about doing any further training or qualifications to help with your next stage of progression?

IF YES

Ask: What sort of areas are you interested in developing?

Allow the learner to explain before recommending a programme.

IF NO / NOT SURE

Move naturally into the AI conversation below.

4

AI Conversation

SDR

One thing we’re speaking to a lot of our previous learners about at the moment is AI.

Out of interest, are you using AI much within your job at the moment?

IF YES
SDR

What are you mainly using it for?

  • Are you mainly using things like ChatGPT, Copilot or other AI tools?
  • Are you using it to save time on everyday tasks?
  • Are you using it for data, reporting or analysis?
  • Are you using it for marketing or content?
  • Are you automating any processes?
  • Are there still manual tasks within your role that AI could help with?
TRANSITION

The reason I ask is that we now offer an AI & Automation apprenticeship, designed around actually applying AI and automation within someone's job rather than just learning AI theory.

It covers using AI tools effectively, improving processes, identifying opportunities for automation and using AI to improve productivity.

Based on what you've just described, it could potentially be quite relevant to your role.

Would you be interested in having a look at some information on it?

IF THEY USE AI A LITTLE
SDR

That’s actually very common. A lot of people are using AI occasionally, but haven't necessarily had structured training around how to use it properly or identify where automation could save them time.

That’s one of the areas our AI & Automation apprenticeship focuses on.

Would you be interested in seeing what the programme covers?

IF THEY DON'T USE AI
SDR

Is that because there hasn't really been an opportunity to use it, or because you're not sure how it could apply to your role?

That’s one of the reasons the programme can be useful. It isn't just aimed at people already working in AI.

It helps people understand where AI can be practically applied within their existing role and organisation.

Would it be useful if I sent you some information so you could have a look?

5

If AI Isn't Relevant

SDR

No problem at all. We've expanded the programmes we offer quite a lot since you originally studied with us.

Depending on what you're doing now, we have progression opportunities across IT and technical support, Cyber Security, Data, Software, Marketing, Leadership and Management, Business and Administration, and AI and Automation.

Is there any particular area that would be useful for your career at the moment?

6

Secure the Information Request & Callback

SDR

I'll send you some information so you can have a proper look at it.

I've currently got your email address as [Email Address].

Is that still the best email address for you?

If incorrect, update the email address.

SDR

Rather than me sending the information and leaving you to it, why don't we arrange a quick call once you've had a chance to look through it?

I can answer any questions and, if you decide you'd like to explore enrolling, we can talk you through the next steps.

8

Referral Conversation

Use this if the learner doesn't want further information.

SDR

No problem at all.

Just before I let you go, we're currently running a referral scheme for previous Velocity Academy learners.

If you know somebody at work or within your network who would benefit from completing an apprenticeship with us, and they eventually enrol, you'll receive a £50 Amazon voucher as a thank-you for the referral.

Is there anyone that immediately comes to mind?

IF YES

Great. The easiest thing is either for you to introduce us by email, send them our referral information, or check with them that they're happy for Velocity Academy to contact them.

Once they've agreed, we would just need their name, telephone number and email address, and we'll take it from there.

Suggested Conversation Flow

Previous apprenticeship→What has happened since?→Same employer?→More responsibility?→Further development?→Using AI?→AI & Automation→Send information→Book callback→Referral if no interest
Key SDR principle: this should feel like a genuine past-learner progression conversation, not “you completed an apprenticeship with us — would you like to do another one?”. The strongest transition is: “Are you using AI much within your job at the moment?”
