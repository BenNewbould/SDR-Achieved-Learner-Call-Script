Nursery Manager Sales Script

Position Playroom Education as the modern, flexible alternative to traditional early years apprenticeship delivery — real human coaching, 24/7 digital learning, embedded AI support and faster Level 3 pathways.

Primary Call Objective

Identify existing nursery employees who need the Level 3 Early Years Educator qualification, particularly where gaining Level 3 would enable them to contribute more effectively towards room ratios or progress within the setting.

Upskill existing staff to Level 3
Support room-ratio capability
Recruit new apprentices if required
Build future L5 / L2 pipeline
Open a wider CPD conversation

Introduce Playroom Education

SDR
The reason I’m asking is that Playroom Education delivers the Level 3 Early Years Educator apprenticeship, but we’ve built the programme quite differently from the traditional model.

We’re part of Velocity Education Group, and our sister organisation Velocity Academy specialises heavily in technology and AI training. We’ve taken a lot of that experience and applied it to early years education.

So rather than early years training feeling like it was designed ten years ago, we’re trying to make it work much more like modern digital learning.
5

Position Playroom as the Modern Alternative

SDR
The biggest challenge we hear from nursery managers isn’t normally that they don’t want to train their staff. It’s that the traditional training model doesn’t always fit nursery life.

You might have somebody booked onto a class, then two members of staff ring in sick and suddenly that learner is needed in ratio. With a very traditional provider, they’ve missed the session and are immediately trying to catch up.

We’ve built Playroom around that problem.
There is still live structured teaching, and every apprentice gets a real human coach supporting them throughout the programme.

Alongside that, learners get 24/7 access to online learning covering the programme content, so if nursery life gets in the way, their learning doesn’t simply stop.
6

Human Coach + AI Coach

SDR
Every Playroom apprentice gets their own human coach. We’re certainly not replacing trainers with AI.

They’ve got a real person who knows them, understands their development and supports them throughout the apprenticeship.

What we’ve done differently is give them an AI coach alongside their human coach.
Imagine one of your practitioners is completing some learning at 8 o’clock in the evening and doesn’t quite understand a topic. Traditionally, they either guess, stop working or wait until the next tutor session.

With Playroom they can ask the AI coach there and then: “Can you explain that differently?” “Can you give me an example from a nursery?” “Can you test my understanding?” “What should I look at next?”

The human coach still provides genuine coaching, assessment, feedback and support — the AI simply means the learner is never left stuck between interactions.
7

Level 3 – Two Pathways

Fast Track – 8 Months

For learners with stronger prior experience and an appropriate starting point.

  • Relevant nursery experience
  • May already hold Level 2
  • Paediatric first aid already in place
  • No functional-skills delivery requirement
Do you have anybody in your team who sounds like they could fit that profile?

Traditional Pathway – 12 Months

More suitable where the learner needs additional development time.

  • Functional-skills support may be required
  • Paediatric first aid not yet held
  • Less experience in early years
  • No Level 2 or limited prior experience
We don’t treat every learner as if they’re starting from exactly the same point. The aim is to place them on the pathway that is appropriate to their actual experience and starting point.

Assessment Without the Traditional EPA Bottleneck

SDR
We deliver the newer version of the apprenticeship standard, so there isn’t the same traditional end-point assessment bottleneck where somebody completes all their learning and then faces one large final assessment event.

Assessment activity is completed throughout the programme, so competence is being built and demonstrated as the learner progresses rather than everything resting on one major hurdle at the end.

Common Objections

“We’re happy with our current provider.”

Completely understandable. We’re not necessarily trying to move a nursery away from something that’s working.

Out of interest, what do you particularly like about your current provider?

The reason I ask is that those are exactly the areas we’ve tried to rethink with Playroom — 8 and 12-month routes, human coaching alongside the AI coach, 24/7 online learning, whole-setting CPD access and the newer assessment model without the traditional EPA bottleneck.

Even if you keep your current provider for some provision, would you be open to comparing what we do for your next Level 3 requirement?

“Our staff don’t have time.”

That’s probably the most common concern we hear from nursery managers — and it’s one of the reasons Playroom was built the way it is.

Learners still have structured live teaching and their human coach, but they also have 24/7 access to online learning. If they’re working independently and get stuck, the AI coach is there to support them.

The training is designed to work around nursery life rather than pretending nursery life doesn’t happen.

“I’m not sure I want AI teaching my staff.”

The important distinction is that AI does not replace the trainer or coach. Every apprentice has a real human coach.

The AI is an additional support tool — more like an assistant between sessions. The learner is still being taught, supported and assessed by real people.

“Why can you complete it faster?”

Because we assess the learner’s actual starting point. Someone with strong early-years experience, Level 2, paediatric first aid and no functional-skills requirement shouldn’t necessarily be treated exactly the same as somebody completely new to the sector.

It isn’t about cutting corners. It’s about not artificially extending training where the learner is capable of progressing faster.

“We don’t need anything right now.”

No problem. Before I let you go, there are three things we’re planning ahead for: Level 5 Early Years Lead Practitioner, Level 2 Early Years Practitioner and wider staff CPD.

Do you think any of those could become relevant to your setting over the next 6–12 months?
